# Phase 1 - Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
Edugenie - Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## 1. Problem Identification

Students may face difficulties while understanding educational
topics and finding quick explanations for their questions.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Edugenie is an AI-powered learning assistant that uses Google Gemini
to provide answers and simple explanations to students.

## 3. Objectives

- To provide quick answers to students' questions.
- To explain educational topics in a simple way.
- To provide an easy-to-use learning assistant.
- To support students in their learning process.

## 4. Target Users

- School students
- College students
- Learners

## 5. Main Features

- Ask educational questions
- Get AI-generated answers
- Simple topic explanations
- Easy-to-use interface
- Clear conversation

## 6. Expected Outcome

Edugenie will provide students with a simple and interactive
AI-based learning assistant for educational support.
